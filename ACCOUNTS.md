## v1 platform: X (Twitter)

**Why X first (not Telegram):** X is a *discovery* platform — its algorithm + reposts
push content to people who don't follow us yet. That's how we grow **new qualified
reach**, which is the 90-day goal. Telegram is a *community* platform (nurture people who
already found us) — it's the downstream conversion layer, added after we have reach to
convert.
