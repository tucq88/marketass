## Product: Oraichain Quant Terminal

**One-liner:** An AI-powered algo-trading terminal on Oraichain where you choose your
level of control — trade fully non-custodial direct on-chain (DEX), *or* deposit into a
performance-backed custody vault. Same AI quant engine either way: agents scan price
behavior across supported pairs hourly, predict entries, and execute (on Lighter DEX).

- **Where it lives:** https://quant.orai.io
- **How money moves:** users deposit USDC; funds tied to their own trading account.
- **Status:** **Live.** Early traction ~800 active users, ~$68M cumulative trading volume.

---

## Who it's for (v1 target)

**Primary (the people we go after for qualified reach): new traders & vault depositors.**
- Active crypto traders who want automation but don't trust black-box custodial bots.
- People who'd deposit into a vault *if* they trusted the performance.

**Secondary (channel, not target): the $ORAI community.**
- They amplify and care about token narrative, but reaching them is mostly *vanity* reach
  unless they actually deposit. We measure success by **depositors/active traders**, not
  token sentiment or follower count.

---

## The buying trigger ✅ confirmed

**They keep losing money FOMO-trading manually** — chasing pumps, trading on emotion,
blowing up their own account — and they want **disciplined, unemotional AI execution**
instead. The pain is *self-inflicted losses from undisciplined trading.*

- **Active path:** "Let disciplined AI run my strategy so I stop FOMOing into bad trades."
- **Lazy path:** "I don't even want to manage it — I'll just join the vault and let it run."

**Core emotional benefit = discipline.** The AI removes the human failure mode (FOMO,
emotion). This is the hook for content: not "trade smarter" — *"stop blowing up your
account on emotional trades; let the machine stay disciplined for you."*

## Positioning / the one thing that makes it worth choosing

> **"You choose your level of control. Go fully non-custodial and trade direct on-chain,
> or deposit into a performance-backed custody vault — same AI quant engine either way.
> Backed by real, transparent performance, not promises."**

**The wedge = spectrum of control + performance-backing.** Most products force one
extreme (scary custodial black box *or* pure DIY DeFi). Quant Terminal offers both ends
and the middle, and the vault is performance-backed — which kills crypto's #1 objection:
*"why should I trust you with my money?"*

**Built-in trust ladder (our funnel):** a skeptical trader can *start non-custodial*
(zero trust required) → watch the engine work → *graduate to the vault* once performance
earns it. We market along this ladder.
